Welcome to My Prayer

Your spiritual companion for daily prayers and Azkar

Accurate Prayer Times

Prayer times for your location, with a calculation method chosen for your region

Daily Azkar

Morning and evening Azkar with counters, a reset button and the virtue of each zekr

Reminders You Control

Turn notifications on and choose exactly which prayers should remind you

🎨

Light & Dark Themes

Pick light, dark or follow your system. It applies everywhere at once

• Keyboard shortcuts support

Get Started

1. Allow location access, or search for your city in Settings (the gear icon in the popup)
2. Notifications are off by default. Turn them on in Settings and choose your prayers
3. Open the sidebar from the popup for the full schedule and your Azkar
